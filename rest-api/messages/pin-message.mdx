---
openapi: post /messages/{id}/pin
description: "Pin a CometChat message in a one-on-one or group conversation with REST API, as the app or on behalf of a user."
---

A conversation can hold up to **100 pinned messages**. Pins made on behalf of a user and pins made as the app share this limit — exceeding it returns `ERR_PINNED_MESSAGES_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`.

The limit is checked without a lock, so two pins sent at the same moment on a conversation that is one below the limit can both succeed and leave it one over. The overshoot is at most one, it appears in the pinned list, and it can be unpinned like any other pin.

For the complete error reference, see [Error Guide](/articles/error-guide).
