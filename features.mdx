CometChat is seven products on one platform. This page lists what each one does and links the
page that documents it, so you can see the whole surface without opening twenty pages.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Chat & Messaging" icon="comments" href="/fundamentals/overview">
    One-on-one and group messaging, media, threads, reactions, search, presence, receipts.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Voice & Video Calling" icon="video" href="/calls/platform/overview">
    One-on-one and group calls, conferencing, recording, transcription, screen sharing.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI Agents" icon="robot" href="/ai-agents">
    Build an agent in the dashboard, or bring your own from Mastra, CrewAI, Agno, Vercel AI, LangGraph, AG2 or AG-UI.
  </Card>

  <Card title="AI Moderation" icon="shield-check" href="/moderation/overview">
    Rule-based and AI moderation, automated blocking, flag and review, custom engines.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Notifications" icon="bell" href="/notifications">
    Push, email and SMS, with preferences, templates and your own provider.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Campaigns" icon="paper-plane" href="/campaigns">
    Scheduled and sequenced outbound messaging to your users, with analytics.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Insights" icon="chart-line" href="/insights">
    Usage, user, conversation and calling analytics.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Build with AI" icon="sparkles" href="/agent-skills">
    Agent Skills, the CometChat MCP server and the CLI, for coding agents.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Notification extensions

`push-notification`, `email-notification` and `sms-notification` are the legacy notification
extensions. New apps use [Notifications](/notifications) instead; see
[Notification extensions](/fundamentals/notification-extensions).

### Legacy moderation extensions

`data-masking`, `profanity-filter`, `image-moderation`, `sentiment-analysis`, `xss-filter`,
`slow-mode`, `human-moderation`, `report-message` and `report-user` are legacy extensions.
Use [AI Moderation](/moderation/overview) instead; see
[Moderation](/fundamentals/moderation-extensions) for the legacy list.

`live-migration-applozic` is a legacy migration extension; see
[Data import and migration](/fundamentals/data-import-and-migration).
