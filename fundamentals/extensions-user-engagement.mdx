## Giphy

GIFs are a great way to change the tone or convey emotions in your conversations.

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ❌ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ❌ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/overview) ❌ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/user-engagement#giphy) ✅ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/user-engagement#giphy) ✅ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/user-engagement#giphy) ✅ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/user-engagement) ❌
</Note>

### Before you begin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Giphy app">
    Sign up at [Giphy](https://developers.giphy.com/dashboard/) and create a new app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select API access">
    Select **API** and click **Next**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your API key">
    Enter your app name and description, create the app, then copy the API key.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Extension settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Giphy">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Giphy**. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your API key">
    Open the extension settings, paste your Giphy API key, and save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Message Translation

The Message Translation extension helps you translate messages into multiple languages.

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ✅ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ✅ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/extensions#message-translation) ✅ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/user-engagement#message-translation) ✅ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/user-engagement#message-translation) ✅ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/user-engagement#message-translation) ✅ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/user-engagement) ❌
</Note>

### Extension settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Message Translation">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Message Translation**. 
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Supported Languages

We support translations in 70+ languages including: English (en), Spanish (es), French (fr), German (de), Chinese Simplified (zh), Japanese (ja), Korean (ko), Hindi (hi), Arabic (ar), Russian (ru), Portuguese (pt), and many more.

<Note>
Language codes follow [RFC 5646](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_ISO_639-2_codes). Use ISO 639-1 codes where available.
</Note>

## Polls

Polls let you quickly record opinions directly in conversations and view the results.

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ✅ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ✅ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/extensions#polls) ✅ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/user-engagement#polls) ✅ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/user-engagement#polls) ✅ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/user-engagement#polls) ✅ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/user-engagement) ❌
</Note>

### Extension settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Polls">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Polls**. 
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Polls are custom messages. Use the `incrementUnreadCount` flag in `metadata` to update unread counters when a poll is received.
</Note>

## Reminders

Create reminders for messages or anything else.

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ❌ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ❌ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/overview) ❌ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/user-engagement#reminders) ✅ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/user-engagement) ❌ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/user-engagement) ❌ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/user-engagement) ❌
</Note>

### Extension Settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Reminders">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Reminders**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the reminder bot user">
    In **Users**, create a user with UID `cc_reminder_bot`. The name and avatar can be of your choice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The `cc_reminder_bot` user must exist for your app. Ensure no existing user uses the same UID.
</Note>

**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ✅ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ✅ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/extensions#stickers) ✅ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅

</Note>

### Extension settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Stickers">

</Note>

## Stipop

Stipop is the world's trendiest sticker platform and creator community.

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ❌ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ❌ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/overview) ❌ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/user-engagement#stipop) ✅ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/user-engagement) ❌ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/user-engagement) ❌ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/user-engagement) ❌
</Note>

### Before you begin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Stipop account">
    Sign up at [Stipop](https://stipop.io/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your app details">
    Provide your application name, website, category, sub-category, region, and app MAU.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your credentials">
    Click **Get Started**, then copy the API Key from the dashboard. In **Settings** → **Application**, copy the Application ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Extension settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Stipop">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Stipop**. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add credentials">
    Open the settings and enter your Stipop Application ID and API Key.
  </Step>
</Steps>
