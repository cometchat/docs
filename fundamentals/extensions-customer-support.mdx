## Chatwoot

The Chatwoot extension makes customer support seamless for your users. Instead of having two interfaces- one for chat between users and one for chat with your support team, you can use CometChat as a front-end for your customer support use case as well!

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ❌ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ❌ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/extensions) ❌ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/customer-support) ❌ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/customer-support) ❌ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/customer-support) ❌ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/customer-support) ❌
</Note>

### Before you begin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Chatwoot account">
    Sign up at [Chatwoot](https://app.chatwoot.com/app/auth/signup) if you don't already have an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete mandatory setup">
    - Add agents: [Chatwoot agent setup](https://www.chatwoot.com/docs/user-guide/add-agent-settings)
    - Create a channel and inbox: [Chatwoot channel setup](https://www.chatwoot.com/docs/product/channels/api/create-channel)
    - You can skip the webhook URL for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your access token">
    Open your avatar menu in the bottom-left, scroll to the Account settings section, and copy the Access token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your Inbox ID">
    Go to **Settings** → **Inboxes**, open the target inbox, and copy the Inbox ID from the URL.
    Example: `https://app.chatwoot.com/app/accounts/123/settings/inboxes/12128` → Inbox ID is `12128`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your Account ID">
    In **Settings**, open **Account Settings** and copy the Account ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Extension settings

#### On CometChat Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Chatwoot">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Chatwoot**. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Chatwoot details">
    Enter your Chatwoot Access token, Account ID, and Inbox ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the support user">
    Enter the CometChat UID for your Customer Support contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save settings">
    Save to generate the webhook URL.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### On Chatwoot Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open inbox settings">
    Go to **Settings** → **Inboxes**, then open the settings for the target inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the webhook URL">
    Paste the CometChat-generated webhook URL into the **Webhook URL** field.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How does it work?

* The end users of your app can send queries to the Customer Support user that you have set in the extension's settings.
* These queries will be forwarded to the configured Chatwoot inbox.
* When an agent replies to the queries, those will be sent over to CometChat and received by your end user.
* With this, your end users can communicate with each other as well as your Customer support team using the same Chat interface.

## Intercom

The Intercom extension makes customer support seamless for your users. Instead of having two interfaces- one for chat between users and one for chat with your support team, you can use CometChat as a front-end for your customer support use case as well!

<Note>
**Available via:** [UI Kit Builder](/chat-builder/react/overview) ❌ | [Widget Builder](/widget/html/overview) ❌ | [UI Kits](/ui-kit/react/extensions) ❌ | [SDK](/sdk/javascript/overview) ✅
</Note>

<Note>
**SDK Implementation:** [JavaScript](/sdk/javascript/extensions/customer-support) ❌ | [Android](/sdk/android/extensions/customer-support) ❌ | [iOS](/sdk/ios/extensions/customer-support) ❌ | [Flutter](/sdk/flutter/extensions/customer-support) ❌
</Note>

### Before you begin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Intercom account">
    Sign up at [Intercom](https://intercom.com) if you don't already have an account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a test workspace">
    Follow Intercom's [test workspace steps](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/188-create-a-test-workspace-in-intercom).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a DeveloperHub app">
    In Intercom settings, open **Developers** → **DeveloperHub** and create an app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the access token">
    Open the app's **Authentication** section and copy the Access token.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The integration works with Intercom API Version `2.3 (2020-11-12)`. If there's a version mismatch, the extension won't work.
</Note>

### Extension settings

#### On CometChat Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Intercom">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Intercom**. 
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Intercom details">
    Enter your **Intercom Access token** and the **Customer Support UID** (CometChat user).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save settings">
    Save to auto-generate a webhook URL for your app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### On Intercom Developer Hub

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the webhook URL">
    Paste the CometChat-generated webhook URL in the **Webhooks** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select webhook topic">
    Choose `conversation.admin.replied` from the Webhook Topics list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How does it work?

The end users of your app can send queries to the Custom Support user that you have set in the extension's settings.

These queries will be forwarded to the Intercom dashboard.

Similarly, messages sent from Intercom dashboard by the support team or admin will be sent over to CometChat and received by your end user.

With this, your end users can communicate with each other as well as your Custom support team using the same Chat interface.
