## Disappearing messages

The Disappearing messages extension allows end-users to send messages that disappear after a certain interval of time. This extension works for both private (one-on-one) and group messages.

Also known as "exploding messages" or "ephemeral messages" on other platforms.

**When to use:** 
- Privacy-sensitive industries (healthcare, legal, finance)
- Compliance requirements for data retention
- Confidential business discussions
- Any scenario where message permanence is a concern

### Extension settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your CometChat app">
    Login to [CometChat](https://app.cometchat.com/login) and select your app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Disappearing Messages">
    Go to **Chat & Messaging --> Features**, under **Extentions** and enable **Disappearing Messages**. 
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How does it work?

Once the messages are sent, you can immediately schedule them for deletion using the disappearing messages extension. The message with the mentioned `msgId` gets deleted at `timeInMS`.

<Warning>
The value of `timeInMS` should be less than 1 year.
</Warning>
