---
title: "How many members can a CometChat group have?"
description: "Group member limits in CometChat, why the 300 figure is a feature switch rather than a performance ceiling, and which features change at which size."
---

Up to 300 members with delivery and read receipts on, and up to 100,000 with receipts off. It is a feature switch, not a performance ceiling: receipts cost per-member work on every message, so switching them off raises the member limit by more than two orders of magnitude. If you need a large group and receipts together, [talk to us](https://www.cometchat.com/contact-sales), because the answer depends on your message rate.

## The published limits

| Property | Value |
| - | - |
| Maximum members per group, all features enabled | 300 |
| Maximum members per group, receipts off | 100,000 |
| Delivery and read receipts, groups | Sent for groups with up to 300 online members |
| Typing indicators, groups | Sent for groups with up to 1,000 online members |
| Unread message counts, groups | Not updated for groups with more than 300 members |
| Presence subscriptions | Up to 1,000 concurrent online users per app |

Two of those rows carry the number 300 and they do not mean the same thing. The first is a cap on how many members a group may hold. The third is a threshold on how many members are online at the same time. They share a number because receipts are the feature that sets the member cap.

## What this does not do

- **It does not raise the limit on request.** The 100,000 figure requires receipts off. There is no plan tier that gives 100,000 members with receipts on.
- **It is not a concurrency figure.** Maximum members is not the same as members online at once. Concurrent connections are part of your plan, not of a group: see [pricing](https://www.cometchat.com/pricing).
- **Unread counts stop before the member limit does.** Unread message counts are not updated for groups above 300 members, so they do not work in a 100,000 member group even though membership does.
- **It does not make receipts selective.** Receipts are on or off for the group. There is no supported way to keep them for a subset of members.

## The app-wide presence ceiling

Presence subscriptions are active for up to 1,000 concurrent online users **per app**, not per group. Beyond 1,000, presence notifications are not sent, across friend, role-based and all-user subscriptions alike. This is an app-wide ceiling rather than a group one, so it applies no matter how you size your groups, and on a busy app it is reached before most of the group limits above.

## Sources

Group member limits, receipts, typing indicators, unread counts and presence subscriptions come from the Users and Groups table on [Properties and Constraints](/articles/properties-and-constraints).

These are server-side platform limits and are not tied to an SDK version.
