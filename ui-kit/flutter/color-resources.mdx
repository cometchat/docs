## Introducing CometChatColorPalette

The `CometChatColorPalette` class allows you to customize the colors used throughout your app. It works the same way in V6 as in V5 — you assign it to the `extensions` property of your `ThemeData`.

Here's what you can customize:

* Primary Colors — Main color scheme of your app
* Neutral Colors — Balanced base for visuals
* Alert Colors — Informative, warning, success, and error messages
* Background Colors — Background shades for different areas
* Text Colors — Text element colors for readability
* Border Colors — Borders for buttons and cards
* Icon Colors — App icon colors
* Button Colors — Button background and text colors
* Shimmer Colors — Loading animation colors

## Ring Screen Colors

On Android, the incoming call ring screen is drawn by `cometchat_voip`, not by the UI Kit, and can appear while your app isn't running. Your `ThemeData` doesn't reach it on its own. `CometChatIncomingCallTheme.from` reads your palette, light and dark, and your app passes it to `cometchat_voip`; see [Styling the ring screen](/ui-kit/flutter/calling-integration#styling-the-ring-screen).

Each ring screen colour comes from one palette colour. When your palette leaves a colour out, the UI Kit's default is used:

| Ring screen field | `CometChatColorPalette` colour | UI Kit light | UI Kit dark | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `background` | `background1` | #FFFFFF | #141414 | Screen background |
| `callerName` | `textPrimary` | #141414 | #FFFFFF | Caller name |
| `subtitle` | `textSecondary` | #727272 | #989898 | Call type line |
| `avatarBackground` | `primary` | #6852D6 | #604CC3 | Initials circle |
| `avatarText` | `white` | #FFFFFF | #FFFFFF | Initials |
| `acceptButton` | `success` | #09C26F | #0B9F5D | Answer button and notification accent |
| `acceptIcon` | `white` | #FFFFFF | #FFFFFF | Icon on the Answer button |
| `acceptLabel` | `textSecondary` | #727272 | #989898 | "Answer" label |
| `declineButton` | `error` | #F44649 | #C73C3E | Decline button and missed call accent |
| `declineIcon` | `white` | #FFFFFF | #FFFFFF | Icon on the Decline button |
| `declineLabel` | `textSecondary` | #727272 | #989898 | "Decline" label |

On iOS, CallKit draws the call, and these colours don't apply.
