Groups help your users to converse together in a single space. You can have three types of groups- private, public and password protected.

Each group includes three kinds of users- owner, moderator, member.

## How many members can a group have?

Up to 300 with delivery and read receipts on, and up to 100,000 with receipts off. It is a feature switch rather than a performance ceiling. See [How many members can a CometChat group have?](/articles/group-member-limits) for the full table, including the app-wide presence ceiling.
